##### Anu Bhaskararaman

Anu is a Bharatanatyam dancer, choreographer, and teacher trained in the Kalakshetra tradition. With over two decades of experience performing and teaching, she brings a deep reverence for classical form together with an openness to contemporary expression.

A disciple of renowned gurus in the Kalakshetra lineage, Anu has performed extensively across India and internationally at festivals, sabhas, and cultural events. Her choreographic work draws on traditional repertoire while exploring new narratives rooted in mythology, poetry, and lived experience.

As founder and artistic director of Lila Arts Centre, Anu is dedicated to creating a space where the next generation of dancers can develop their art with rigour, joy, and creative freedom.
