##### Anu Bhaskararaman

Anu is a Bharatanatyam dancer, choreographer and teacher, and the founder and
artistic director of Lila Arts Centre. She performs and teaches here and
elsewhere — most recently the varnam *Shri Krishna Kamalanatho*, under the
auspices of Natyarangam.

At Lila she is building a space where the next generation of dancers can
develop their art with rigour, joy and creative freedom.
